## Notae v0.11.0

Notae 0.11.0 turns a set into something you can play from start to end, keeps the tablet's navigation in one place, and fixes the search order and a round of small things found in a review of the whole app. It updates v0.10.0 in place (same app id, `com.c2yz.notae`) and keeps your library, favorites, sets, notes and drawings.

### Added
- **Playing a set.** Turning past the last page of a piece opens the next piece of the set, and turning back from the first page opens the previous one at its last page. The viewer shows the set and the place in it under the title („Msza niedzielna · 2 of 5”), names each piece as it opens, and says when you reach the start or the end of the set.
- **Add pieces, calmer.** The add picker no longer opens the keyboard over its list, shows each score's first page, and with no search lists the scores you opened recently first.
- **Empty states with a next step.** An empty list of sets offers New set and an empty set offers Add pieces (on a phone the floating button does, so the card does not repeat it); All scores says Notae is looking for your scores while the first scan runs.

### Changed
- **One navigation.** On a tablet the side panel no longer turns into a row of chips when it is hidden or the details are open: a navigation rail with the sections and Settings takes its place, as in portrait. Composers moved out of the panel into a composer filter above the scores on All scores; search keeps its Composers scope.
- **Composers, one person each.** A composer field such as „Daniel Gardner; opr. Marcin Pospieszalski” lists each person in the composer filter, without „opr.”, „sł.”, „muz.” and similar; text sources such as „z Liturgii godzin” are left out.
- **Settings.** The choices come first, then Library maintenance, then About. The counts have plainer names (number conflicts, files that could not be read, PDFs without searchable text), each with a line on what it means. Scan library moved here from the tablet's header, since Notae scans on its own when the library opens.
- **Clearer labels.** „From desktop” reads „From the catalogue”; inside a set the score's number reads „No. 16”, so it is not taken for its place in the set; Open score shows a page rather than an external-link arrow unless scores open in another app; the set's date field reads „Choose a date”.
- Reorder is off until a set has two items, and Share until it has a score.

### Fixed
- **Search order.** All ranks a match by the field it is in: the start of the title, then a word of the title, the composer, the tags, and the lyrics last. Titles that match equally well keep the number order instead of the shortest title first.
- **Search results while typing.** A longer query that brings better matches to the top no longer leaves them scrolled off above the list.

### Verification
- **Gates:** `scripts/check.sh full` and GitHub CI (Rust and Gradle gates: lint, unit and screenshot tests, the release build). New tests cover turning between pieces of a set, the field ranking and number order in search, the composer split, the recent scores in the add picker, the rail and the composer filter, and the new empty states.
- **Checked on an Android 15 tablet emulator** (2560 × 1600) with the schola library: playing a set, the rail beside the details and in portrait, the composer filter, search, Settings and the add picker.
- **Checked on an Android 15 phone emulator** (Pixel 7, 1080 × 2400) with the same library, upright and on its side: the first scan, the ⋮ menu, Settings, a new set, the add picker, playing a set, the composer sheet and search.
- **Not tested on a physical tablet** or on an Android 8 device.
