## Notae v0.9.0

Notae 0.9.0 lets you choose how the viewer shows your pages, zooms anywhere on the page, keeps the notes beside the score on a tablet, and takes the whole library from a ZIP file or a link, with no folder to pick.

It updates v0.8.0 in place (same app id, `com.c2yz.notae`) and keeps your library, favorites, sets, notes and drawings.

### What's new
- **Import scores from a ZIP.** On the welcome screen, or in **Settings → Library folder**, tap **Import scores from a ZIP**:
  - **Choose ZIP file** picks one on the device.
  - **From a link**: paste the address (for example the one your choir sent) and tap **Open in browser**. The steps tell you what to tap; in Google Drive it is ⋮ → Download. Then come back and tap **Choose ZIP file**, or share the downloaded file to Notae.

  A ZIP shared to Notae, or opened with it, imports too; when a library is open, Notae first asks whether to replace it. The PDFs are unpacked into Notae's own storage, so Android asks for no folder or SD-card access. Importing a newer ZIP later replaces the scores and keeps your favorites, sets, notes and drawings on the ones that are still there. Choosing a folder again deletes Notae's copy. Notae still has no internet access: your browser downloads the file.
- **View menu** (the open-book button in the viewer):
  - **Layout**: **Pages**, turned as before, or **Scroll**, pages one under another as in a PDF reader.
  - **On screen**: **1 page** or **2 pages**.
  - **Scale**: **Fit page(s)**, **Fit width**, **Fit height**, or 50–200 % of the printed size.

  Notae remembers your choice on the device. Until you choose, the viewer behaves as in 0.8.0.
- **Notes beside the score.** On a tablet in landscape, **Notes** opens as a panel on the right, so the score stays in view. On narrower screens it is the sheet from below, as before.
- **Zoom anywhere.** Pinch works over the whole page, the edges and drawings included, and never turns the page.
- **Page turns slide.** A swipe follows your finger. Edge taps, arrow keys and page-turn pedals turn with the same animation.

### Fixed
- A new library whose scores reuse the numbers of the previous one no longer reports those scores as failures until the next scan.
- Very large pages (200 %, or zoomed at fit width) render within a memory budget instead of crashing.
- In Scroll, the page counter reaches the last page; a lone last page of two on screen keeps the size of the others.
- While the pen draws, a hand resting on the glass no longer zooms the page.

### Known limitations
- Notae cannot download by itself, by design: a link opens in your browser.
- Pinch zoom and the notes panel were tested in automated tests and on an emulator, but not yet with real fingers on a physical tablet. They are on the 0.9.0 device checklist.

### Verification
- **Gates:** GitHub CI runs the Rust gates and the Gradle gates (lint, unit and screenshot tests, the release build). New tests cover the viewer's layouts, scales, zoom and memory budget, the notes panel and View menu, unpacking a ZIP (PDFs and `.notae/` kept, other files skipped, unsafe paths rejected, Polish Windows names, no PDFs, no space, re-import), reading the ZIP library and handing its PDFs to another app, and the import dialog and questions.
- **On an emulator (Android 15 tablet):**
  - A ZIP picked in Settings became the library: 13 scores, 0 failures.
  - The scores opened in Notae's viewer and in the system PDF app.
  - **From a link** opened the browser.
  - Sharing a ZIP from Files asked before replacing the library.
  - A changed ZIP replaced one score and kept a favorite.
  - Switching back to a folder worked.
