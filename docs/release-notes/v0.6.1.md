## Notae v0.6.1

Notae now moves the way a current Android app does: screens slide, dialogs and sheets open and close smoothly, and lists animate as they change. This release also fixes screens that looked wrong in v0.6.0, mostly in dark mode.

It updates v0.6.0 in place (same app id, `com.c2yz.notae`) and keeps your library, favorites and sets.

### What's new
- **Screens slide.** Opening a set, the details, Search or Settings slides the new screen in. Back slides it away, and the back gesture shrinks the screen as you swipe. Switching sections fades.
- **Dialogs and sheets animate.** Sheets rise from the bottom and dialogs grow in. Drag a sheet down to close it.
- **The details move with you.** On a tablet the details pane slides in beside the list. **Previous** and **Next** slide the details in the direction you went, keeping your scroll position.
- **Lists animate.** Scores and sets glide into place when they are added, removed or reordered. Thumbnails fade in, and grid and list fade into each other.
- **Phones:** the large title folds or unfolds completely when you lift your finger, and **New set** and **Add** grow in and out.
- **Haptics** when you pick up, move and drop a row in reorder mode, and when you star a score in the details. They follow your phone's touch-feedback setting.
- Everything follows Android's **Remove animations** setting.

### Fixed
- **Dark mode:** the details' info card is no longer a black hole in its card, and pages without a thumbnail are no longer bright grey blocks.
- **Tablet in portrait:** the details stack the page, the title and the info instead of leaving the bottom of the screen empty.
- The welcome screen is centred on tall screens. Settings' language rows are no taller than its other rows.
- Launching the app shows its own background colour instead of a flash of another.
- **Back** closes the composer list on a phone instead of leaving the app.
- In reorder mode, a dropped row no longer jumps back before it moves, and moving the first row no longer scrolls the list.

### Verification
- **Gates:** GitHub CI runs the Rust gates and the Gradle gates (lint, unit and screenshot tests, the release build). New tests cover dialogs closing, dragging a sheet, dropping a reordered row, the large title, haptics and the portrait details. The screenshot goldens were re-recorded for the dark-mode and layout fixes.
- **Not verified on a device or an emulator.** Screenshot tests cannot show motion. Please report anything that looks or feels wrong.
- **Not yet verified on the Galaxy Tab S10 FE+**, as for v0.6.0.

### Install
Download `notae-0.6.1-arm64.apk` and its `.sha256`, and verify the signature as described in the README's **Install** section. The signing certificate is the same as for every earlier release.
