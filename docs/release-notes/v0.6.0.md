## Notae v0.6.0

Notae now runs on Android phones as well as tablets. The layouts follow Samsung's One UI: the controls you use most sit in the lower half of the screen, within reach of your thumb.

It updates v0.5.0 in place (same app id, `com.c2yz.notae`) and keeps your library, favorites and sets.

### What's new
- **Phones** (windows narrower than 600 dp):
  - A bottom bar with All scores, Recent, Favorites and Sets.
  - A large title that folds into the toolbar as you scroll. The toolbar has Search and a ⋮ menu with Scan library, grid or list, and Settings.
  - A composer filter that opens a list of composers at the bottom of the screen.
  - **New set** and **Add** as floating buttons, and **Done** at the bottom of the screen while reordering.
  - The details show the page, the title, **Edit details** and **Add to set**, then the info with each label above its value. **Previous**, **Open score** and **Next** sit in a bottom bar.
  - Edit details and Add pieces take the whole screen. Other dialogs float at the bottom of the screen.
- **Small tablets and phones on their side** (600–840 dp): a navigation rail with the sections and Settings, and the composers as chips.
- **Tablets:** the same two-pane layout as v0.5.0. Dialogs now use One UI's text buttons split by a divider, and the side panel's rows are pill-shaped.

### Changed
- The app no longer declares itself tablet only, so phones can install it.
- Settings group titles are in the accent colour. On a phone, the last scan's summary goes below its label.

### Verification
- **Gates:** GitHub CI runs the Rust gates and the Gradle gates (lint, unit and screenshot tests, the release build). New tests cover the phone layout at 384 × 832 dp, and the screenshot goldens include phone captures.
- **Not verified on a device or an emulator.** The phone and rail layouts were checked only by the Robolectric tests and screenshots. Please report anything that looks wrong on your phone.
- **Not yet verified on the Galaxy Tab S10 FE+**, as for v0.5.0.

### Install
Download `notae-0.6.0-arm64.apk` and its `.sha256`, and verify the signature as described in the README's **Install** section. The signing certificate is the same as for every earlier release.
