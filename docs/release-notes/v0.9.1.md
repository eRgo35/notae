## Notae v0.9.1

Notae 0.9.1 fixes small things in the viewer that came with 0.9.0. It updates v0.9.0 in place (same app id, `com.c2yz.notae`) and keeps your library, favorites, sets, notes and drawings.

### Fixed
- **Zoom range.** A pinch reaches from half the fit page to four times the printed size, whatever scale you chose. At 200 % it stops at 400 %.
- **Two-finger tap.** A still two-finger tap at the edge of the page no longer turns it.
- **1 or 2 pages.** Switching from 1 to 2 pages keeps the page you are on: on page 4 you see the 3–4 spread and the counter stays at 4, and back on 1 page you are still on page 4.
- **Pinch in Scroll.** The point under your fingers stays there also across, not only down.
- **New layout.** A layout or scale picked in the View menu starts at its scale from the first frame, without a flash of the old zoom.
- **Smoother pinch.** Pinching moves the pages without composing them again on every frame.

### Known limitations
- Pinch zoom was tested in automated tests and on an emulator, but not yet with real fingers on a physical tablet.

### Verification
- **Gates:** GitHub CI runs the Rust and Gradle gates (lint, unit and screenshot tests, the release build). New tests cover the zoom range, the two-finger tap, the page kept from 1 to 2 pages, the point kept across in Scroll and the pages not composed during a pinch.
- **On an emulator (Android 15 tablet):** 1 → 2 pages kept page 2 of 2; Scroll scrolled down and up; at 200 % in Scroll the page panned across, sharp.
