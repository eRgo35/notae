## Notae v0.8.0

Notae now opens scores in its own viewer, lets you draw on the scores in a set, scans your library almost instantly, and fits its screens better on phones, foldables and tablets of every size.

It updates v0.7.1 in place (same app id, `com.c2yz.notae`) and keeps your library, favorites, sets and notes.

### What's new
- **Notae's own PDF viewer.** Tapping a score opens it in Notae. On a tablet in landscape two pages show side by side (1–2, 3–4, …); in portrait one page fits the screen; on a phone on its side pages fill the width and scroll. Tap the left or right edge, swipe, or use the arrow and Page Up/Down keys to turn. Pinch to zoom and double-tap to fit again. Tap the middle of the page to hide or show the bar. The screen stays on while a score is open.
- **Move through a set.** Opened from a set, the viewer has **Previous piece** and **Next piece** (skipping text notes and pieces missing from your library) and **Notes**, which shows the score note and the set note and lets you edit them.
- **Draw on the scores in a set.** Tap **Draw** to use a pen, a red pen, a highlighter, an eraser or text boxes, with **Undo**, **Redo** and **Clear page**. Once Notae has seen a stylus, only the stylus draws: a finger or a resting palm moves the page. Drawings belong to that set (the same score in another set is clean), never change the PDF, and can be hidden. If the PDF changes later, the viewer warns that drawings may not line up. Removing a piece with drawings from a set asks first.
- **Settings → Open scores in.** Choose **Notae** or **System default app** (the earlier behaviour). After the update a one-time hint says where to change it.
- **Share with drawings.** When you share a set, tick **Include drawings** to send them along. **Export sets** now includes your drawings.
- **Almost instant scans.** Notae scans when you pick a folder and when it starts, and skips files whose path, size and modified time have not changed. The first scan after updating reads every file once.
- **PC sidecar.** The new `notae-sidecar` tool reads `Indeks.csv` and the PDFs on a PC and writes `.notae/` into the library folder; Notae then takes titles, numbers, composers, tags, lyrics and thumbnails from it, so even the first scan of a large library is fast. Fields you edited on the tablet keep your edit.
- **Layout fixes.** Short windows (a phone on its side, split screen) get a slim side rail; details, the welcome, the add picker and the metadata editor keep their titles and buttons in view; screen titles stay on one line; the library grid fits full rows, with three columns on foldables and small tablets; a tablet keeps both panes when the keyboard opens.

### Known limitations
- Exports and shared sets with drawings use a new file version that 0.7.x cannot read. A shared set without drawings stays readable by 0.7.x.
- Downloading a library from a URL is planned for 0.10.0.

### Verification
- **Gates:** GitHub CI runs the Rust gates and the Gradle gates (lint, unit and screenshot tests, the release build). New tests cover the viewer (page pairing, turning, zoom, keys, Previous/Next, notes, the hint, the changed-PDF warning), drawing (tools, eraser, text, undo, stylus and palm handling with real pen events, saving when leaving or when the app stops), storing drawings per set item, backups and shared sets with drawings, and the scan. Screenshot tests capture every key screen and the viewer across a matrix of phone, foldable and tablet sizes.
- **Not verified on a physical device:** the viewer and drawing with a real S Pen (palm rejection, the eraser end, drawings staying in place after rotation) and the new scan on the full library. They are on the 0.8.0 device checklist.
