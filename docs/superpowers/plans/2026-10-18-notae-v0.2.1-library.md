## #26 Open on tap, Info icon

- [x] Tap, Enter/Space and the accessibility default action on a card or row emit `open()` in every section (All, Recent, composer).
- [x] Replace the pencil with one 48 px **Score details** (info) action opening the full-width preview; no edit icon on cards or rows.
- [x] Editing metadata only from the preview's **Edit details**; the long-press menu offers **Open score** and **Score details**.
